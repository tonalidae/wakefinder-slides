How do we detect 3D velocities?
  • 9.6 square degree field of view
  • The Moon covers 0.2 degrees
  • 4300 images to cover the southern hemisphere sky.
  • Takes 5 nights (12 years for other observatories).
  • Larger data volume (1000 times more information)
  • 100x deeper.

Data analysis

  • N-body simulations. Code GADGET-4
  • 1 million particles.
  • 2 datasets:
    1. Initial state, unperturbed halo
    2. After the interaction with the LMC
  • Data: 3D positions and velocities with their id
  • Histograms: position, and angular momentum computed from the particle velocities and their id.
  • Classification into low and high angular momentum regions

Conclusions
    
    The Magellanic Cloud induces a dark matter wake 
    in the Milky Way halo due to dynamical friction.

    The Vera Rubin Observatory will provide an 
    unprecedented 5D map of the Milky Way stellar halo.
    (positions and proper motions) 
    These data will be ideal for detecting the dark 
    matter wake

    Stars associated with the dark matter wake can 
    be detected by their change in angular momentum.
